## 🗣️ Voice and Tone Guidelines

### Product Voice
How should the product always sound?

- [ ] **Professional** - Knowledgeable and trustworthy
- [ ] **Friendly** - Approachable and warm
- [ ] **Direct** - Clear and to-the-point
- [ ] **Playful** - Fun and energetic
- [ ] **Empowering** - Confident and supportive

*Choose 2-3 that fit your product*

### Tone by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| First time use | Encouraging, clear | "Let's get started! This will take about 2 minutes." |
| Error state | Helpful, calm | "Connection failed. Check your internet and try again." |
| Success | Positive, brief | "Dataset uploaded!" |
| Empty state | Motivating, informative | "No pipelines yet. Create one to get started." |

### Words We Use / Avoid

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid | Why |
|--------|----------|-----|
| "Create" | "Provision" | Simpler, more common |
| "Delete" | "Terminate" | Less technical |
| "Settings" | "Configuration" | Friendlier |

### Uncertainty Handling
How will the AI express confidence/uncertainty?

- **High confidence:** 
  *Example: "Based on your data, sales increased 23% in Q4."*

- **Medium confidence:** 
  *Example: "It looks like sales increased, but I'd recommend verifying the exact percentage."*

- **Low confidence:** 
  *Example: "I'm not sure about that. Would you like me to connect you with support?"*

### Error Recovery
What happens when the AI fails?

*Example: "I didn't understand that. Could you rephrase your question? Or type 'help' to see what I can do."*

### Human Handoff
When should users talk to a real person?

*Example: Offer human support when AI fails 2+ times or user explicitly requests it*

## ♿ Accessibility Checklist

- [ ] **Plain language:** Written at 8th grade reading level or lower
- [ ] **Alt text:** All images have descriptive alt text
- [ ] **Link text:** Links describe destination ("View pricing" not "Click here")
- [ ] **Headings:** Proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
- [ ] **Error messages:** Clearly state problem and solution
- [ ] **Color:** Don't rely on color alone to convey meaning
- [ ] **Abbreviations:** Spelled out on first use
- [ ] **Jargon:** Technical terms explained or avoided

## 🛡️ Ethical AI Checklist

*Complete if your feature uses AI*

### Transparency
- [ ] Users know they're interacting with AI
- [ ] AI capabilities and limitations are clear
- [ ] AI explains its reasoning when appropriate

### Accuracy
- [ ] AI responses are fact-checked
- [ ] Sources are cited when relevant
- [ ] Confidence levels are communicated

### Fairness
- [ ] Content reviewed for bias
- [ ] Tested with diverse users
- [ ] Works for users of different backgrounds

### Safety
- [ ] Harmful content is filtered
- [ ] Users can report problems
- [ ] Human review available for sensitive topics

### Privacy
- [ ] Data usage is explained
- [ ] Users can control their data
- [ ] Retention policies are clear

### Accountability
- [ ] Clear who's responsible for AI behavior
- [ ] Users can appeal AI decisions
- [ ] Feedback loop exists for improvement

## ✅ Review Checklist

Before launching, verify:

### Clarity
- [ ] Key message is immediately clear
- [ ] Instructions are specific and actionable
- [ ] No jargon without explanation
- [ ] Examples provided where helpful

### Consistency
- [ ] Terminology matches style guide

- [ ] Formatting is consistent
- [ ] Links work correctly

- [ ] Facts are verified

## 🏢 Benchmark Against Industry Guidance

Before writing, check which published guidance applies to your feature. Note what you'll adopt and where you'll differ.

| If your feature has… | Benchmark against | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| Error states | [Microsoft error guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/debug/error-message-guidelines), [Adobe Spectrum](https://spectrum.adobe.com/page/writing-for-errors) | Does every error say what happened and what to do? |
| An AI assistant or chat | [Microsoft Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/) | Does the first screen say what it can do and how well (G1, G2)? |
| AI that can fail or be unsure | [Google People + AI Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/chapter/People%20+%20AI%20Guidebook%20-%20Errors%20+%20Graceful%20Failure.pdf) | Which AI error types can happen, and what's the path forward for each? |
| AI-generated content in the UI | [IBM Carbon AI label](https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/ai-label/usage/) | Is every piece of AI content marked, with an explanation one click away? |
| AI-generated images or media | [Adobe Content Credentials](https://helpx.adobe.com/in/creative-cloud/apps/adobe-content-authenticity/content-credentials/overview.html), [EU AI Act Article 50](resources/ethical-ai/transparency-guidelines.md) | Does output carry machine-readable provenance? |
| An agent that takes actions | [Agentic AI Experiences](templates/ai-content/agentic-experiences.md) | Is there a plan, approval, progress, and receipt for each action? |
| A global audience | [Shopify app content guidelines](https://shopify.dev/apps/design-guidelines/content) | Is it about a US grade 7 reading level, with no idioms? |
| A voice interface | [Amazon Alexa situational design](https://developer.amazon.com/alexa-skills-kit/situational-design) | Does it make sense by listening alone? |

All 10 companies' guidance: [How Leading Companies Design Content for AI](resources/industry-examples.md)
